# Brag Plan: Guardian Copilot (PayPal AI Hackathon 2026)

## What is this app?
Guardian Copilot is an autonomous financial agent for PayPal that protects a sacred $500 safety reserve floor, models 90-day cash flow with decimal precision, searches 25,000+ stores on Channel3, routes risky payments to PayPal Pay in 4, and visualizes real-time ledger telemetry in high-performance AG Grid tables.

## The angle
"Shopping AI agents execute payments without checking if rent is due Friday. Guardian Copilot simulates, models, and protects your money before capture."
It flips the paradigm from blind autonomous spending to proactive liquidity defense with Dual Human-In-The-Loop approval.

## Hook (first 10 seconds)
A dramatic problem-and-solution hook on deep navy `#04060c`:
- Problem: Most AI commerce agents spend blindly until an overdraft hits.
- Solution: Guardian Copilot defends a non-negotiable $500 cash reserve floor, reroutes purchases to Pay in 4, and enforces Dual HITL security.

## Key moments (the walkthrough)
1. **Act I — Channel3 Live Price Intelligence & PayPal Checkout** (0:10 - 0:34):
   - Universal product search across 25,000+ retailers.
   - Real-time 90-day price history curve and multi-store offer comparison.
   - Direct execution in PayPal Sandbox with instant settlement confirming the $500 reserve floor is intact.
2. **Act II — Copilot Multi-Agent Swarm & PayPal REST v2 Invoicing** (0:34 - 1:00):
   - Natural language conversational intent: "Create an invoice for a merchant with gmail example848@gmail.com for $350..."
   - LangGraph consensus swarm (Router, Scraper, Memory, Trader, Auditor, Arbiter) evaluates liquidity and generates a REST v2 Draft PayPal Invoice card.
   - Dual HITL approval gate: Non-debiting receivable dispatch with Q3 Liquidity & Risk Canvas telemetry.
3. **Act III — Financial Telemetry & AG Grid Invariant Defense** (1:00 - 1:18):
   - Real-time PayPal Sandbox ledger powered by AG Grid with sortable/filterable transaction records and Pay in 4 status badges.
   - Rolling 90-day cash flow trajectory strictly holding the $500 sacred floor line.
4. **Act IV — System Architecture & Tech Stack** (1:18 - 1:32):
   - Architecture diagram: LangGraph Swarm + Decimal Financial Arbiter + PayPal REST v2 + Channel3 SDK + AG Grid + Next.js 15 + FastAPI + Render.
5. **Act V — Closing Title Card & Call to Action** (1:32 - 1:40):
   - Guardian Copilot glowing shield logo, "Simulate, shop, and transact before you spend", PayPal AI Hackathon 2026, GitHub repo.
