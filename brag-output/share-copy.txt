# PayPal Guardian Copilot — Devpost & Social Launch Copy

## 🏆 Devpost Submission Copy (Overview & Pitch)

### Tagline
Autonomous financial safety copilot that guards liquidity floors, evaluates purchases via Channel3, and automates PayPal REST v2 orders and invoicing with human-in-the-loop protection.

### Elevator Pitch
Every year, consumers and businesses incur billions in overdraft fees and liquidity crunches due to blind, single-click spending. PayPal Guardian Copilot transforms traditional checkout into an autonomous financial co-pilot. Powered by a multi-agent swarm (Trader, Auditor, Arbiter) running deterministic invariant logic, Guardian evaluates 25,000+ merchants via Channel3, projects 90-day cash flow against a sacred $500 liquidity reserve floor, suggests Pay in 4 options when full payment would breach reserves, and securely executes PayPal REST v2 Orders and Invoices with cryptographic idempotency and Human-in-the-Loop approval gates.

### How It Works
1. **Universal Multi-Merchant PDP (Channel3)**: Browse real-time products across 25,000+ merchants with live price-history charts and multi-seller offer comparisons.
2. **Financial Invariant Engine**: Before every stage or checkout, Guardian projects cash flow across a 90-day deterministic horizon. If paying in full violates the $500 reserve floor, Guardian automatically gates the transaction and suggests an interest-free PayPal Pay in 4 installment plan.
3. **Copilot Multi-Agent Swarm**: Natural language interface allowing users to inspect cash reserves, analyze vendor quotes, or dispatch PayPal REST v2 invoices. A 3-agent consensus debate halts risky debits and presents clean Bento action cards.
4. **Live PayPal REST v2 Sandbox Settlement**: Direct OAuth2 integration with PayPal Sandbox endpoints (`/v2/checkout/orders` and `/v2/invoicing/invoices`), supporting live capture and invoice settlement.
5. **Real-Time Telemetry & AG Grid Ledger**: High-performance AG Grid financial ledger tracking all completed transactions, recurring SaaS commitments, and dynamic liquidity projections.
